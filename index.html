FAQ

Straight answers.

What exactly counts as a "booked job"?
An appointment on your calendar for an in-service-area estimate or install, confirmed by phone with a real prospect. Not a form fill. Not a click. Not a "lead."
What happens if you don't hit 6 booked jobs?
As long as you've met the agreed minimum ad spend and answered calls on the agreed response-time SLA, we pay you 3x every dollar you paid us in setup and management fees. In writing, before the pilot starts.
Do you control my ad budget?
No. Ad spend runs through your own ad accounts and is billed to you directly by the platform. Our fees only cover building and running the pilot — we never touch or mark up your ad dollars.
How long am I locked in?
90 days. This is a defined pilot, not a retainer you get stuck in. The goal is proof — a real cost per booked job — before either of us talks about anything longer.
Do you work outside the Philadelphia metro?
Right now the pilot is focused on Philadelphia metro service areas, so cost per booked job stays tight and manageable. Outside that area, reach out and we'll tell you straight whether we can help.
